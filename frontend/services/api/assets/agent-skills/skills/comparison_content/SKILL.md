# Comparison and alternatives content

## Goal

A comparison that helps a real buyer decide. The sponsoring brand may be the best choice, a narrow-fit choice or the wrong fit for some needs; say so honestly.

## Where the data is

- `get_project_business_context`: the brand's offers, prices and named competitors.
- `read_visibility_results`: how AI answers compare the options today.
- `read_performance` and `read_search_dataset`: the comparison searches buyers make.
- Competitor facts from a cited web search or the person, dated.

## What good looks like

- The same criteria for every option, chosen by what matters to the buyer, not trivial ones the sponsor wins.
- Prices keep currency, billing period and add-ons. Unknown is not "missing", "free" or "worse".
- "Choose X if…" for each option, including the sponsor's real limits; without evidence for a verdict, give a factual table and conditional guidance.
- A buyer who picks the competitor should feel fairly represented. Attribute reported experiences; never invent ratings or tests.

## Deliver

One document: the page (title, decision summary, a comparison table of supported facts, the differences that matter, when to choose each option, an honest call to action), then editorial notes listing each claim's source and date and the facts to recheck.
