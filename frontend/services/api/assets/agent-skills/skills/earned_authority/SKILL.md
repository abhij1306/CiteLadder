# Earned mentions, backlinks and source opportunities

## Goal

A short list of attainable, relevant third-party opportunities with usable drafts. A few well-chosen prospects beat a list of famous domains.

## Where the data is

- `read_visibility_sources` and `read_source_url`: the pages AI answers cite, and the brands each one lists.
- `read_search_dataset`: referring-domain totals, not individual links; never describe a linking page or anchor from them.
- `get_project_business_context`: the facts and assets worth pitching.

## What good looks like

- A live backlink, an unlinked mention, a cited source naming the brand and an independent recommendation are different outcomes.
- Judge topic fit, audience and whether inclusion is realistic before a provider authority score, which is an estimate.
- Pick one move per prospect: correction, editorial inclusion, link reclamation, expert contribution, original asset or community answer.
- No bought links, fake reviews or covert edits; paid sponsorship is labelled. Sending and posting are the person's actions.
- Name the contact route to look for (author page, contribution guidelines); never guess names or emails.

## Deliver

One document: a prospects table (source, type, what was observed, proposed action, destination page, priority reason), one complete draft per selected prospect, and rejected prospects with a one-line reason.
